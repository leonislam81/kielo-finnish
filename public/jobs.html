FINLAND’S JOB LANDSCAPE

Where are the broadest opportunities?

The Helsinki metropolitan area is Finland’s biggest job hub. Other cities have distinctive industry clusters; the best fit depends on your field, language and experience.

Read Work in Finland’s labour-market guide ↗
⌖
Broadest employer mixHelsinki · Espoo · Vantaa

A large metro area with employers across many industries.

⚙
Technology & industryTampere · Oulu · Vaasa

Regional strengths include ICT, manufacturing, health technology and energy.

✺
Distinctive local sectorsTurku · Kuopio · Lapland

Maritime, life sciences, research, tourism and regional services.

How to read this: These are regional strengths, not a live ranking of open jobs. Vacancies change daily; check the original listing.

Break a new job market into manageable steps, from choosing a role to preparing for an interview.

Read official job-search advice ↗

Choose a target

Pick a role, sector and one or two realistic search areas.

02

Describe your skills

Connect your experience to the terms employers use in Finnish and English.

03

Prepare your application

Tailor your CV and short application to the exact vacancy.

Check requirements

Confirm language, work eligibility and whether your qualification needs recognition.

05

Apply and track

Save the original advert, closing date and your next action in My applications.

06

Prepare for an interview

Practise a short introduction and a few Finnish phrases, even if the interview is in English.

WHEN YOU GET AN OFFER

Check the details before your first day.

Use these official guides to understand what you are agreeing to and where to get help.

✎

Read the contract

Check duties, dates, pay, hours, holidays, probation, collective agreement (TES) and notice period. Ask if anything is unclear before signing.

Contract guide ↗
◷

Understand pay & hours

Sector agreements affect pay, shifts and overtime. Keep your own record of hours and compare them with your work schedule.

Pay and working-hours guide ↗
€

Check your tax card

Review your tax card and income ceiling in MyTax. Update your estimate when your pay or work situation changes.

Tax Administration guide ↗
✳

Know your rights & safety

Find guidance on equal treatment, workplace safety, occupational health care and where to ask about employment terms.

Employee rights guide ↗

This is a starting checklist, not legal or tax advice. Your contract and circumstances matter; ask the relevant authority or a qualified adviser about personal questions.
