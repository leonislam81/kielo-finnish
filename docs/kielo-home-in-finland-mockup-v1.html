A SIMPLE STARTING ROUTE

Find and rent a home, step by step.

Providers set their own application requirements. A signed written agreement is your anchor.

BEFORE YOU SIGN
01
Set your full monthly budget

Add rent, water, electricity, insurance, internet, transport and any parking or sauna fee.

02
Search by commute and daily needs

Search student housing providers, city rental services and private listings. Some landlords ask for identity and proof of income or funds; some may also ask for a residence permit or Finnish personal ID. Requirements vary.

03
Check the home and the offer

Confirm address, condition, heating, included costs, house rules and the landlord. Do not send a deposit before you have checked the written agreement and payment details.

04
Read and sign the written lease

Check fixed-term or open-ended dates, rent, deposit, notice, utilities and all named tenants.

05
Pay as agreed; record the condition

Keep receipts, photograph the home at move-in, arrange keys, electricity and home insurance.

Deposit: usually two months' rent and legally no more than three months. It cannot be used as the final month's rent. Always follow the signed agreement and confirm the payment destination.

FOR STUDENTS ARRIVING FROM ABROAD

Start the housing search early.

Your school, housing provider and Migri each handle different parts of the process.

01
Accept your study place

Check the school's housing page and ask its student services where to apply.

02
Apply to student housing right away

In larger cities it can take weeks or months; keep a private-rental backup plan.

03
Prepare documents and a realistic budget

Provider requirements vary. Keep your admission, identity and funds documents ready.

04
Check residence and family guidance separately

Use current Migri rules for your own permit. Property or a rental contract does not replace immigration steps.

Student housing is often cheaper, but it is not guaranteed. Availability, eligibility and family apartment rules belong to each provider.

BEFORE YOU MAKE AN OFFER

Know what you are buying.

In Finland, an apartment and a detached house can follow different ownership, cost and permit rules.

🏢

Housing-company shares

Often an apartment or terraced home. Compare selling price and debt-free price, monthly fees, company debt and planned repairs.

asunto-osake · housing-company share

🏡

Real estate

A detached house and plot are commonly real estate. Check title, zoning, plot, condition, property tax and all maintenance costs.

kiinteistö · real estate

🧾

Transfer tax estimate

Typical current rates: 1.5% for housing-company shares; 3% for real estate. Different bases and deadlines apply.

Rough transfer-tax estimate€2,700

Preliminary only. Verify the tax base and due date with Vero.

🌍

Buying as a newcomer

Many non-EU/EEA citizens need a Defence Ministry permit to buy real estate. Housing-company shares generally follow a different rule. Check your case before a deal.

Buying a home does not grant a residence permit or citizenship.

DREAMING OF A PLACE YOU DESIGN?

Building starts with the plot and the local plan.

Before buying land, ask the municipality's building-control authority what your project can actually do.

01 · FindPlot & locationCheck ownership or lease, access, services and year-round transport.
02 · VerifyZoning & building rightsAsk about the local detailed plan, restrictions and permitted use.
03 · BudgetFinance & professionalsInclude design, construction, connection fees, loan costs and contingency.
04 · PermitMunicipal building controlConfirm current permits, plans, notices and requirements before work begins.
05 · BuildInspect & completeFollow inspections, contracts, utilities and final documentation.
Projects and local plans differ. Even if a particular small structure does not need a permit, it must still follow applicable regulations. Ask municipal building control first.

A HOME PURCHASE, IN ORDER

Take time to check the whole cost.

Buying can involve a lender, seller, estate agent, housing company, tax authority and land register. Confirm each step for the property you choose.

01 · PrepareTalk to a lenderAsk what financing may be available and what monthly payment fits your budget.
02 · CompareCheck the true priceFor a housing-company share, review the debt-free price, monthly charges, company accounts and repair plan.
03 · InspectUnderstand conditionReview documents, visit the home and consider an independent inspection for a detached house.
04 · AgreeRead before signingUnderstand the offer, sale contract, conditions, ownership shares and any required permit.
05 · CompletePay, report, registerVero sets the transfer-tax return and payment steps; real-estate title registration has its own deadline.
For a home bought together, agree in writing how ownership shares, loan payments and ongoing costs are divided. A lender or legal professional can explain the contract for your case.
