A SHORT TIMELINE

Many chapters shaped one country.

A starting point for Finland’s story—from earlier rule and autonomy to independence and modern partnerships.

BEFORE 1809Part of the Swedish realm

The region that is now Finland was governed as part of Sweden for centuries.

1809Autonomous Grand Duchy

Finland became a Grand Duchy of the Russian Empire, developing its own institutions.

6 DEC 1917Independence

Finland’s Parliament approved the declaration of independence.

1919A republic

The independent country became a republic under its new constitution.

1995 · 2023European & Nordic partnerships

Finland joined the European Union in 1995 and NATO in 2023.
