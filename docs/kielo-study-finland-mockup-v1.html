KEEP YOUR PREVIOUS LEARNING VISIBLE

Credits, recognition and transfers

Moving between degree programmes can be possible, but it is never automatic. Ask the target institution before planning around transferred credits.

60
ECTS

What does a credit mean?

A full-time academic year is typically 60 ECTS credits. Credits represent student workload. A Finnish institution decides how a course or prior learning fits a specific degree.

Read the national system guide ↗

Can I transfer credits or change universities?

Sometimes. A transfer application may exist for a particular programme and intake. Conditions can include a current Finnish study right, completed credits, progress, language, available places and remaining study time.

  • Ask the receiving programme about transfer eligibility and deadlines.
  • Prepare an official transcript, course descriptions and learning outcomes.
  • Request a written credit/RPL assessment; recognition is decided by the institution.
  • Check whether accepting a transfer changes your current study right.
How academic recognition works ↗
Important: If you are changing from a foreign institution, an assessment of your previous education for admission and the recognition of individual credits towards a degree are related but separate decisions.
