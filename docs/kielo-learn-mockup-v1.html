12 QUICK QUESTIONS · PRE-A1 TO C2

Already know some Finnish?

Get a suggested place to start. It’s a practice guide, not an official exam.

Find my starting level →

A short check can only suggest a starting point. Official CEFR or YKI results require a recognised assessment.

Can I start as a complete beginner?

Yes. Start at Pre-A1 with the sounds and short phrases; no Finnish knowledge is needed.

How does my progress stay saved?

Your lessons and word review are saved in this browser. You can export a backup in the learning app.

Can I learn at my own pace?

Yes. Short lessons and flexible daily word goals let you decide how much to do today.
