FINLAND’S JOB LANDSCAPE

Where are the widest opportunities?

The Helsinki metropolitan region is Finland’s biggest job hub. Other cities have strong specialist clusters — the right fit depends on your field, language and experience.

Read Work in Finland’s labour-market guide ↗
⌖
Broadest employer mixHelsinki · Espoo · Vantaa

Large metro area, many industries and international employers.

⚙
Technology & industryTampere · Oulu · Vaasa

Strong regional clusters in manufacturing, ICT, health tech and energy.

✺
Distinctive local sectorsTurku · Kuopio · Lapland

Maritime, life sciences, research, tourism and regional services.

How to read this: these are regional strengths, not a live ranking of open jobs. Vacancies change daily; check the linked marketplace and employer advert.

WHEN YOU GET AN OFFER

A good next step is to check the details.

Before your first workday, use these official guides to understand what you are agreeing to and where to get help.

✎

Read the contract

Check duties, workplace, dates, pay, hours, holidays, probation, collective agreement (TES) and notice period. Ask for an explanation before signing if anything is unclear.

Contract guide ↗
◷

Understand pay & hours

Learn how your sector agreement affects pay, shifts and overtime. Keep a record of the hours you work and compare them with your rota.

Check your tax card

Review your tax card and income ceiling in MyTax. Update your estimate if your pay or work changes; your individual tax rate depends on your situation.
