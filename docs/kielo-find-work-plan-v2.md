# Kielo · Find work in Finland — Plan v2

**Status:** Research-backed clickable mockup; not the production page yet  
**Research checked:** 4 November 2026

## Product direction

Turn Find a Job into **Find work in Finland**: an English-first career and employment guide for people who are new to Finland. The page helps visitors decide where to search, which job marketplaces suit their field, what a city is known for, how Finnish language expectations can vary, and what steps to take before applying.

The page is not a live job-board aggregator. It will not display invented job counts, salary ranges, or vacancy cards. It will direct visitors to current source pages and explain what those sources are useful for.

## Page map

1. **Hero and job-search planner** — choose a field, city and language comfort; get relevant city context and recommended job-search sources.
2. **Where to look in Finland** — explain that the Helsinki metropolitan region has the broadest overall job hub, then show specialist city and regional clusters.
3. **City guide** — selectable profiles for Helsinki region, Tampere, Turku, Oulu, Vaasa, Kuopio and Rovaniemi/Lapland; each includes verified sector strengths, a practical fit note, language considerations and related official links.
4. **Job marketplaces by goal** — separate guidance for English/international roles, general job searches, public sector, professional roles, staffing/temporary work, and students/graduates.
5. **A practical job-search route** — role and skills, language plan, CV/application, work eligibility and qualification recognition, application/interview tracking.
6. **Finnish for work** — useful job-search words and a short phrase with audio, linked to Kielo’s existing lessons.
7. **After the offer** — a plain-English checklist for the contract, pay and working hours, tax card, work rights and safety, with links to the responsible authorities.
8. **Reliable next steps** — official employment guidance, work-rights information, credential recognition and job-seeker safety.

City profiles describe established regional strengths, not a guarantee that a particular person will find work there. Job opportunities change over time. The mockup makes that distinction visible and uses links rather than fixed open-vacancy counts.

## Marketplace guide

- **English and international searches:** Work in Finland; LinkedIn Jobs; employer career pages. A platform’s English interface does not mean every vacancy accepts English.
- **Broad searches across private employers:** Duunitori; Jobly; Indeed Finland.
- **Municipal and wellbeing-services work:** Kuntarekry; individual city and wellbeing-services employer pages.
- **State and public administration:** Valtiolle.fi; Job Market Finland.
- **Staffing, temporary and service roles:** Barona, StaffPoint, Eezy and Bolt.Works. Show the relevant source, not a promise of a specific opening.
- **Students, graduates and research:** Aarresaari, The Hub, Academic Work, university career services and employer internship pages.
- **Cross-border European opportunities:** EURES, linked as an official portal; its reuse terms do not permit scraping/republication by ordinary visitors.

These recommendations are starting points, not rankings. The original vacancy controls the requirements, language, deadline and application process.

## After an offer: essential checks

- **Read the written contract before signing:** confirm the employer, duties, work location, start date, fixed/indefinite term, probation, pay basis and pay date, hours, holidays, collective agreement (TES) and notice period. Ask the employer to explain anything unclear.
- **Understand hours and pay:** compare the agreed hours with the actual rota, learn how overtime and shift allowances are handled, and keep your own record of hours. Check the applicable collective agreement because pay and terms vary by sector.
- **Sort out tax:** check your wage-earner tax card and annual income ceiling in MyTax; update it if your estimate changes. Kielo should link to the Tax Administration rather than calculate or promise an individual tax rate.
- **Know where to ask for help:** check work eligibility with Migri, workplace safety and employment terms with the occupational safety and health authority, and use official employee-rights guidance for disputes or concerns.

This is an orientation checklist, not legal or tax advice. Personal situations and current rules must be checked with the official authority or a qualified adviser.

## Interaction in this mockup

- Choose a city to update the city profile.
- Select a work goal to filter relevant marketplaces.
- Submit the short planner to show a suggested starting route.
- Tick a private checklist; the browser keeps the selections on this device.
- Play a Finnish phrase through an installed browser voice, where available.
- Navigate to existing Kielo pages and official employer/service links.

## Research and implementation guardrails

- Keep site navigation to one site-language selector (English/Finnish); do not make “explain in” a global setting. Help-language choice belongs inside Finnish lessons.
- Label city sector insights as regional context; never call them live demand or live vacancies.
- Keep vacancy details, work eligibility, credential recognition, salaries and deadlines linked to their current responsible source.
- Do not show sample job listings as if they were live vacancies.
- Do not suggest that English is accepted unless the original vacancy says so.
- The Finnish phrase audio is a browser voice preview, not a promise of a particular male/female voice on every device.
- Keep the application checklist local unless accounts or cloud sync are explicitly designed later.
